        Offline alerts

        When a screen stops checking in, the CMS can post a message to a web address (a "webhook"), e.g. a Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams or Google Chat channel, or an ntfy.sh topic for phone notifications. It posts again when the screen comes back.
